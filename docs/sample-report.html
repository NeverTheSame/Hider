Over-redaction

Recall alone is easy to game: redact everything and score 100 percent. The corpus therefore includes 1000 lines that contain no sensitive value at all, drawn from the same templates. A redaction there is unambiguously wrong.

0 of 1000 clean lines were touched, a false-redaction rate of 0.0%. Getting there needed two named suppression rules, both found by this measurement rather than by inspection: stock IP matching flags build numbers such as 8.1.0.7, and public Microsoft endpoints are not customer data. Both live in one small, tested function, because suppression is the only part of the engine that removes protection.

Why tiering, in money

Tier 1 runs first as a cost gate. It has no per-call price and no network hop, so every character it clears is a character no paid tier is billed for. At an assumed managed-NER list rate of $0.001 per 1000 characters:

TierCharacters offeredCharacters redactedCost at assumed rateWall time

On this corpus tier 1 clears 39.0% of the characters before anything paid is consulted. The saving scales with how shaped the data is, and support bundles are almost all shaped, which is the whole argument for putting regex in front rather than reaching for a model first.

What this does not show

  • The corpus is synthetic. Real bundles hold placements nobody thought to generate. The mitigation is procedural: entity formats were written from public documentation and sanitized structural templates before any recognizer existed, and the hostile corpus above exists to attack the overlap that remains. It is a mitigation, not a fix.
  • One person wrote both sides. Generator and recognizers came from one mental model, so independence here is procedural rather than organisational. Recall on shaped types should be read as an upper bound.
  • Free-text names and organisations are not detected at all in phase 1. That is 564 planted values passing straight through, and it is the entire reason a managed NER tier is phase 2 rather than optional.
  • Prompt-injection resistance is untested. The 5 injection canaries are already in the corpus, but tier 1 has no instructions to subvert, so it cannot be injected and reporting a pass would be dishonest. That number arrives with tier 3.
  • The cost figures use an assumed list rate, not an invoice. Characters offered per tier are measured; the dollar conversion is arithmetic on a published price.
